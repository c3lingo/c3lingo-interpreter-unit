# Housing and 3D Printed Parts

## Housing
The housing is made from bend sheet metal and designed in FreeCAD.

### Requirements
- FreeCAD 1.x with SheetMetal Workbench Addon installed

## Assembly Helpers
To place the level meter (aka. VU meter) LEDs at the needed height, an assembly helper is available at [./assembly-helpers/vumeter-leds.scad](./assembly-helpers/vumeter-leds.scad).

## Euro Crate Inlay
To securely transport the device with headphones a simple inlay for an Auer ED 43/27 HG euro crate with lid was designed.
It consists of a wodden divider (9mm multiplex) at the center of the crate which is screwed in from the outside.
The back compartment can be used for the headphones, the front compartment for the interpreter desk.

To hold the device in place, the lid should have 10mm foam glued on and two side pieces need to be cut from stiff foam, e.g. Plastazote.
See [./c3lingo-eurocrate-inlay-rail_left.pdf](./c3lingo-eurocrate-inlay-rail_left.pdf) for a technical drawing of the left side.
The right side is a mirror image of that.

It's also advised to put 10mm foam on the bottom of the crate and the divider does not go all the way to the bottom otherwise.
